Ocean Exploration

Oceans cover a large portion of our planet and contain ecosystems that are still being explored by scientists.

Deep Sea Exploration

Exploring the deep ocean requires specialized equipment because sunlight disappears as depth increases.

Submersibles

Submersibles allow researchers to travel to depths that would be difficult or impossible to reach by ordinary diving.

Research Equipment

Cameras, sensors and robotic arms can be used to observe and collect information from the seafloor.

Ocean Ecosystems

Marine ecosystems contain many different organisms that depend on one another for survival.

Coral Reefs

Coral reefs provide habitats for thousands of marine species and are among the most diverse ecosystems in the ocean.

Open Ocean

The open ocean contains organisms ranging from microscopic plankton to some of the largest animals on Earth.
