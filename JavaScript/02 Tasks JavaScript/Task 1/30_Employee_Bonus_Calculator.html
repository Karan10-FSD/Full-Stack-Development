Task 30 — Employee Bonus Calculator
Create variables for:

Employee salary
Years of experience
Performance rating
Calculate the bonus using these rules:

Experience ≥ 5 years and rating ≥ 8 → 20% bonus
Experience ≥ 3 years and rating ≥ 7 → 15% bonus
Experience ≥ 1 year and rating ≥ 6 → 10% bonus
Otherwise → No bonus
Display:

Salary
Bonus Percentage
Bonus Amount
Final Salary
